I-KEY

Universal sliding door hook.
As your child grows, the hook grows with them.

Concept Overview

Tidy habits and independence,
starting at a child's eye level.

I-KEY is a universal sliding door hook designed around a child's eye level and everyday behavior. Conventional over-the-door hangers are set at adult height, making it hard for children to hang up or tidy their own clothes. I-KEY solves this everyday inconvenience and helps children naturally build tidy habits and independence from an early age.

Design & Features

01

Height-adjustable sliding mechanism

The hook slides smoothly up and down to match a child's height and stage of growth, so it can be used from childhood into adulthood.

02

Child-friendly safety design

A curved form factor with rounded edges prevents injury on contact, and an intuitive hook structure lets little hands hang and remove items with minimal effort.

03

Harmony with any space

It simply hangs over the top of a door with no drilling or installation, and its minimal design and soft colors blend into any interior.

User Experience & Value

A space where children can do it themselves.

I-KEY goes beyond simple storage to create 'a space where children can do it themselves.' The small success of hanging up their own coat or bag at their own eye level after coming home gives children a sense of achievement and independence, while offering the whole family a more convenient, tidier home.

  1. STEP 1

    Hang it on the door

    Just hang it over the top of the door. No drilling or installation needed.

  2. STEP 2

    Set the height

    Slide the hook up or down to match the child's height.

  3. STEP 3

    Hang it yourself

    Items go on and off with little effort, so children hang their own coats and bags.

  4. STEP 4

    Grow together

    Move it higher as they grow — from childhood all the way to adulthood.
